Is the Comfortably Numb preset free?

Yes. It is one of StompLab's three always-free demos. Open it and download the .hlx without an account or card.

It is built for HX Stomp and HX Stomp XL. In the app you can also export it for Helix Floor, Helix LT, and POD Go (.pgp).

It is a researched starting point, not a guaranteed copy. Your guitar, pickups, monitoring, and hands change the result — expect to fine-tune.
