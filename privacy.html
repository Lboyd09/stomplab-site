Who we are

StompLab is a web app for researching song rigs onto Line 6 units. Live product: stomplab.app. Support: stomplab1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Email sign-in — your email to authenticate an account, free custom-research quota, and subscription status.
  • Subscriptions (Polar) — monthly ($6.99) or yearly ($75) plans processed by Polar as merchant of record. Manage or cancel via Account → Manage subscription.
  • AI song research — song titles and artists you research are processed through the product AI gateway. You do not provide a Gemini API key.
  • Product usage — ordinary telemetry to operate the service. We do not sell personal data.
  • Demo path — Teen Spirit, Sandman, and Comfortably Numb work with no account.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask visitors for a Gemini (or other) API key.
  • We don’t invent fake testimonials, user counts, or press logos on this marketing site.
  • We don’t claim Line 6 / Yamaha affiliation.

Retention

Account email and subscription entitlements are kept while your account exists and as needed for billing records. Contact us for account deletion.
