Privacy

Short version: we collect what we need to run sign-in, subscriptions, and song research — not a surveillance product. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

Stomp Lab (StompLab) is a web app by Liam Boyd. Live product: stomplab.app. Support: stomplab1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Email sign-in (auth) — your email to authenticate an account. Used for free custom-research quota (3 after sign-in) and subscription status.
  • Subscriptions (Polar) — monthly ($6.99) or yearly ($75) plans are processed by Polar as merchant of record. Polar handles billing details; we receive entitlement signals so we can open any song, Create, History, and Gear locker, and apply the 50 custom builds per calendar month. Manage or cancel via Account → Manage subscription in the app.
  • AI song research (Gemini via product gateway) — song titles/artists you research are sent through the product’s AI gateway to map tracking/live rigs onto Line 6 models. You do not provide a Gemini API key.
  • Product usage — ordinary telemetry to operate the service (demos opened, device selection, errors). We do not sell personal data.
  • Demo path — Teen Spirit, Sandman, and Comfortably Numb work with no account. If you never sign in, we don’t need your email.
  • Amazon affiliate — Gear locker / shop links for original gear only may be Amazon affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We do not receive your Amazon password or full payment card from those clicks.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask visitors for a Gemini (or other) API key.
  • We don’t invent fake testimonials, user counts, or press logos on this marketing site.
  • We don’t claim Line 6 / Yamaha affiliation.

Third parties

  • Hosting — app and marketing site (commonly Vercel).
  • Auth — email authentication used by the live app at stomplab.app.
  • Polar — subscription checkout, tax, and MoR handling.
  • AI gateway / model providers — process research prompts for rig mappings (including Google Gemini behind the gateway).
  • Amazon — optional affiliate links for original gear referenced in Catalog / Gear.

Retention

Account email and subscription entitlements are kept while your account exists and as needed for billing records. Research inputs may be processed and temporarily logged to operate and improve the product. Contact us for account deletion.

Contact / deletion

Email stomplab1@gmail.com. For receipts, cancellation, or refunds, use Account → Manage subscription / Polar’s customer flow — Polar is the merchant of record.

Changes

If data practices change materially, we’ll update this page. The live app at stomplab.app and /guide remain the source of truth for how research and plans work.
