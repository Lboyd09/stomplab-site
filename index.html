Good questions

Before you plug in.

Still wondering? Email support.

Does StompLab make an exact copy of the record?

No. It creates research-based starting points, not guaranteed 100% copies. Your guitar, pickups, monitoring, room, and playing all change the result. Treat the preset as a strong first draft and adjust it on your rig.

Which Line 6 units are supported?

HX Stomp and HX Stomp XL are battle-tested with .hlx files. Helix Floor, Helix LT, and HX Effects are selectable. HX Effects omits amp and cab blocks. POD Go exports a real .pgp file.

What can I try without an account?

Smells Like Teen Spirit, Enter Sandman, and Comfortably Numb are always free. Open and download them without an account. Sign in for three free custom builds.

What does the subscription unlock?

Subscribers can research any song and use Create, History, and Gear locker. Both paid plans include 50 custom builds each month. Demo presets never count toward that limit.

Do I need my own Gemini API key?

No. Research runs through the StompLab product gateway. Visitors are never asked to paste in a Gemini key.

How do I import a preset?

Download the file and import it in HX Edit or POD Go Edit. For HX hardware, use Snapshot mode for song sections or Stomp mode for effect bypass controls.

Can I cancel the subscription?

Yes. Open Account in StompLab and choose Manage subscription. Polar is the merchant of record and handles checkout and billing management.

Is StompLab affiliated with Line 6 or Yamaha?

No. StompLab is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Line 6 or Yamaha Guitar Group.
