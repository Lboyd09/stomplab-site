The hang-up

Tone-chasing shouldn’t mean drowning in HX Edit.

CustomTone haystack

Hundreds of user presets named after the song — few match the actual tracking chain, cab, or section changes.

Wrong snapshots

A “good tone” with one footswitch still leaves you rebuilding verse / chorus / solo by hand on FS1–FS3.

Guesswork on the unit

You know the record. Mapping amps, pedals, and IRs onto HX models — and hearing them in context — is the slog.

How it works

Demo or research → play on-screen → import.

  1. 01

    Demo or research

    Open a free demo, or sign in and research a custom song. The product’s AI Gateway maps the tracking/live rig onto HX models — no visitor API key.

  2. 02

    Play the replica on-screen

    The screen is the unit. Tap song sections and switches. Guitar or Bass mode. Nudge for FRFR, amp, headphones, monitors, or PA.

  3. 03

    Import .hlx + Snapshot mode

    Download the .hlx. In HX Edit: File → Import. Put the unit in Snapshot mode so FS1–FS3 recall song sections.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it an exact copy of the record?

No. Rigs are research starting points — not a 100% copy of the record. Use them as a reference, then tweak on your unit. The soft disclaimer is in the product for that reason.

Which Line 6 units work?

Built for HX Stomp and HX Stomp XL first. In the app you can also select Helix Floor, Helix LT, HX Effects, and POD Go. Prefer Stomp / Stomp XL if you want the path we’ve battle-tested hardest.

Do I need a Gemini API key?

No. Research runs through the product’s AI Gateway. You don’t paste a visitor Gemini key anymore.

What is Snapshot mode?

After you import the .hlx in HX Edit (File → Import), put the unit in Snapshot mode so FS1–FS3 recall song sections (verse / chorus / solo style parts) instead of only toggling effects.

Does it do bass?

Yes — Guitar and Bass modes. There’s also a playback nudge for FRFR, amp, headphones, monitors, or PA so the cab/IR choice matches how you’re listening.

What’s free vs paid?

Three demos forever with no account. Sign in for three free custom songs. After that, subscribe $6.99/month or $75/year (about $8.88 saved vs monthly). Subscription opens any song, Create, History, and Gear. Each custom research counts toward 50 builds this month — demos never do.

Is it a subscription?

Yes — monthly or yearly via Polar in the app. There is no one-time unlock. Cancel or manage billing through the Polar checkout / customer flow tied to your purchase.

Refunds?

Checkout and billing are handled by Polar as merchant of record. Check the Polar checkout / receipt flow for refund and cancellation terms — we don’t invent a policy here beyond what Polar shows.
