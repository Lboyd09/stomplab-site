How it works

Demo or research → play replica → import file.

  1. 01

    Demo or research

    Open a free demo, or sign in and research a custom song. The product AI gateway maps the tracking/live rig onto factory HX model strings — no visitor API key.

  2. 02

    Play the replica

    The screen is the unit. Tap song sections and switches. Guitar or Bass mode. Nudge for FRFR, amp, headphones, monitors, or PA.

  3. 03

    Import the file

    Download .hlx or .pgp. In HX Edit: File → Import, then PAGE to SNAP/STOMP. Snapshots = song sections; Stomp = effects on/off.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it an exact copy of the record?

No. Rigs are research starting points — not a 100% copy of the record. Use them as a reference, then tweak on your unit. The soft disclaimer is in the product for that reason.

Which Line 6 units work?

Battle-tested on HX Stomp and HX Stomp XL (.hlx). Also selectable: Helix Floor, Helix LT, HX Effects (no amp/cab), and POD Go (.pgp for POD Go Edit — not a fake .hlx).

What about POD Go?

POD Go is a first-class selectable unit. Exports are real .pgp files for POD Go Edit. We do not ship fake Helix .hlx files for POD Go.

Do I need a Gemini API key?

No. Research runs through the product’s AI gateway. You never paste a visitor Gemini key.

Snapshot vs Stomp mode?

After import (File → Import in HX Edit), PAGE to SNAP or STOMP. Snapshot = song sections on FS1–FS3 (FS1 is top-left, matching HX Edit silkscreen). Stomp = effects on/off.

What’s free vs paid?

Three demos forever with no account. Sign in for three free custom songs. Then subscribe $6.99/month or $75/year (save $8.88). Subscription opens any song, Create, History, and Gear locker. 50 custom builds per calendar month — demos never count.

Subscription or one-time?

Subscription only — monthly or yearly via Polar. There is no one-time unlock. Manage or cancel anytime via Account → Manage subscription (Polar MoR).

Refunds & Polar?

Checkout and billing are handled by Polar as merchant of record. Use Polar’s customer flow for receipts, cancellation, and refund terms — we don’t invent a policy beyond what Polar shows.
